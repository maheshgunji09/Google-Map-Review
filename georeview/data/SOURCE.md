# India boundary provenance

The India feature was extracted without coordinate modification from Natural Earth's 1:10m Admin 0 Countries GeoJSON on 25 November 2026. The input feature has ADMIN equal to India. The ingestion audit records the bundled file's SHA-256 fingerprint.

[Source GeoJSON](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson)

Natural Earth data is public domain. Its country geometry is a cartographic dataset with its own treatment of disputed boundaries. Geographic eligibility in this project follows that geometry and excludes points within 0.01 degrees of its edges. This conservative choice can exclude valid coastal, island and border venues. It cannot certify the correctness of an archive's coordinates or address.
